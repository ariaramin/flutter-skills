# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, welcoming place for everyone. Participation must be free from harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, professional, and considerate in issues, discussions, reviews, and pull requests.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and documentation—not people.
- Respect differing experiences, viewpoints, and technical approaches.
- Protect privacy and do not share personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, repeated bad-faith disruption, and any other conduct that makes participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s security-reporting channel when available. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other action needed to protect the community.
